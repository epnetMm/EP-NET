How Mining Works on EPNET

Mining is the process of solving complex mathematical problems using computational power. When you mine, your computer:

  1. Collects pending transactions from the network
  2. Assembles them into a block
  3. Computes a valid proof of work by finding a nonce value
  4. Broadcasts the new block to the network
  5. Receives a mining reward in EP cryptocurrency
Mining requires computational resources. This process will utilize your CPU. It's recommended to monitor system resources while mining.
